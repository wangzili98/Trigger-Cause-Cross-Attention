# Example data

Four sample-scale records are extracted from the dataset supplied by the author, by the author's request. See `provenance.json` for original NPZ filenames, row indices and SHA-256 hashes. The source archive does not contain full event inventories, coordinates, regional grids, later observations or private path information.

Meteorological and disaster data originate from the providers identified in the manuscript (China Meteorological Administration and Department of Natural Resources of Sichuan Province). The MIT license in this repository applies to the software and does not grant rights to the providers' full underlying datasets. Provider access conditions remain applicable. The example retains the source feature encodings and does not infer unprovided physical units.

These May 2025 samples are used only in the small training/I/O demonstration. They are not a new independent validation split and must not be used to substantiate the manuscript's reported skill.
